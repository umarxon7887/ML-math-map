ML MATEMATIKA ILOVASI (PWA)

1) Saytga joylash (bepul, 1 daqiqa):
   - https://app.netlify.com/drop ga kiring va shu papkani (yoki zip ichidagi hamma fayllarni) sudrab tashlang.
   - Tayyor havola beriladi. Uni Telegram guruhga yuboring.

2) Telefonga o'rnatish:
   - Android (Chrome): havolani oching, menyu (3 nuqta) -> "Install app" / "Ilovani o'rnatish".
   - iPhone (Safari): Share -> "Add to Home Screen".

3) Har kimning natijasi alohida: belgilar faqat o'sha odamning telefon/brauzerida saqlanadi.

4) APK kerak bo'lsa: havolani https://www.pwabuilder.com ga kiriting -> Android -> Download.
